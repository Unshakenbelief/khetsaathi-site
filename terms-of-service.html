2. What KhetSaathi is

KhetSaathi is a farm-management app for farmers and their designated land-management contractors in Chhattisgarh, India. It helps track agricultural land parcels, cross-check them against government land records, monitor crop status via satellite data, record expenses, and estimate mandi (market) income.

The App is under active development. Some features described in the app or its marketing are partially implemented or pending — notably, native voice input is not fully live on all platforms, and offline support is not yet wired up end-to-end.
