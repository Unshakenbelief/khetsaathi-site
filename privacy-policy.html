KhetSaathi — Privacy Policy

किसान का सच्चा साथी — A farm-management app for farmers in Chhattisgarh, India

Draft notice: this page is grounded in what the KhetSaathi app actually does (checked against the live code), not generic boilerplate.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the KhetSaathi mobile app (Android/iOS, package/bundle ID in.khetsaathi.app) and the khetsaathi.in website. KhetSaathi is built for farmers and their designated land-management contractors ("अधिया") in Chhattisgarh, India, to track land records, crop status, expenses, and government land-verification data, in Hindi.

Data Fiduciary (DPDP Act term) / Data Controller: KhetSaathi, Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, India.

3. How we use this data

  • Operating the app's core features: land tracking, government-record verification, crop advisory, expense tracking, and contractor access-sharing.
  • Security: detecting and preventing unauthorized account access.
  • Diagnostics: identifying and fixing crashes/errors.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

5. Cross-border data transfer

As shown in §4, coordinate data (not linked to your name/phone/account) is sent to EU-based Open-Meteo and Copernicus Sentinel-2, and prompts may be sent to US-based Anthropic when that AI route is active. Everything else — account data, land records, financial data, crash logs — is processed by India-based providers.

6. Data retention

We retain your account and land/expense data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the app (Manage → Access → Delete account). Deletion is immediate: your login and profile are removed right away, and if you're the owner of your group, every land, contractor, and expense record in that group is deleted with it. If you're a manager/viewer added to someone else's group, deleting your account removes only your own login. There's no retention window beyond standard database backup rotation.

7. About contractor data

If you (a farmer or group admin) add a contractor's name and mobile number, you're providing another person's personal data to us. You're responsible for having a lawful basis to share it.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to access, correct, or request erasure of your data; withdraw consent; and raise a grievance.

To exercise these rights, contact our Grievance Officer: KhetSaathi Support Team, support@khetsaathi.in.

9. Children

KhetSaathi is intended for adult landholders and their contractors. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, Supabase's row-level-security-based access control) to protect your data. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll update the effective date below when this policy changes, and for material changes, we'll notify users in-app.
