किसान का सच्चा साथी — A farm-management app for farmers in Chhattisgarh, India

What KhetSaathi is

KhetSaathi is a mobile application that helps farmers and their designated land-management contractors ("अधिया") in Chhattisgarh keep track of their agricultural land records, crop status, expenses, and government land-verification data — all in one place, in Hindi.

What the app does

  • Tracks land parcels (खसरा records), area, and ownership details
  • Matches land records against official government sources (Bhuiyan / BhuNaksha)
  • Monitors crop stage and satellite crop-health signals
  • Records farm expenses and estimates mandi (market) income
  • Lets a farmer share access with contractors managing their land

Why we use OTP (one-time password)

OTP is used strictly for account login and security. When a user registers, logs in, recovers a forgotten password, or changes their registered mobile number, we send a one-time password to their phone to verify their identity before granting access to their account and farm data.
